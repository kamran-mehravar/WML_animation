# TIRAMISÙ — Production Specification (Working Canon)

## Purpose and tone

Professional product-documentary animation telling the story from laboratory preparation through data/logistics engineering, Scarlino dock setup, and deployment at 30 m. Italian speech, English subtitles where required. Seven recurring character likenesses based on user-approved image references: Bruno, Monica, Kamran, Michela, Nicola, Angela and Fabrizio.

## Characters (approved lineup and roles)

- **Bruno:** sensor firmware, sensor engineering, database design support. Short hair and glasses; do not revert to the early long-haired draft.
- **Monica:** team coordination, waxing, labeling; revised tailored trousers/body proportions.
- **Kamran:** software/database architecture, quality control, tags and sensor integration. Height **187 cm**, close to Nicola in visual scale.
- **Michela:** sensor sleeves with oil filling/sealing, tags. Relatively short in lineup.
- **Nicola:** waxing, bottle tagging and preparation. Tallest or close in height to Kamran.
- **Angela and Fabrizio:** project managers, dock and marine operation coordination. Their paired studio render is an approved independent asset.
- All seven should stand on the same ground line in group shots, not staggered front/back.

## Physical assets and visual locks

- Neutral / generic wine bottles (avoid producer brands unless real software footage already shows one).
- Bottles destined for sea use **green tags**; cellar controls **blue**; sensor bottles **yellow**, with yellow tags also identifying baskets; red tags refer to other testing categories. Follow the specific current scene's protocol and approved assets.
- Tall, bottle-neck-sized wax seal, not a short cap; wax styles and sensor packing must match actual photographic references.
- Sensor sleeve is thin and visibly **oil-filled**, not empty and not shaped like a specimen/urine bag.
- Physical basket: terracotta-colored plastic carrier, approx **35 × 30 × 40 cm**, **12 bottle slots**.
- Cage: user-approved silver open-top collapsible wire-mesh pallet cage, geometrically and dimensionally consistent. Do not rotate it upside down.
- Confirmed laboratory scene with stainless tanks, wine equipment, **Sincera 2** wax apparatus and terracotta tiled floor.
- Scarlino quay, approved compact workboat with open cargo deck and hydraulic crane; no logos on objects or clothing.
- Seafloor environment at target depth **30 m**, muted blue-green lighting, suspended particles and realistic visibility.

## Production chain

1. Generate individual 3D-animated shots from locked first-frame visual references, preserving appearance and environments.
2. Review each shot for shape, scale, geometry, camera stability and continuity, including character facial identity.
3. Record/mix spoken Italian lines against the approved screenplay, optionally English subtitles.
4. Composite the **original** institutional partner banner in the upper-left except during software reveal.
5. Edit approved real application capture directly; don't synthesize a false UI. Keep operator identity and internal URL redacted.
6. At end, animate the partner banner from upper-left into the center and enlarge it. The WML logo belongs to the software reveal, **not** the film end card.
7. Produce Full HD H.264 MP4 at 30 fps, plus editable project and versioned assets.

## Safety and fidelity

- No credentials, VPN details, operator usernames, backend hostnames/IPs in public or presentation frames.
- Real demo data are a *partial current snapshot*, not fully stocked scientific protocol. Production cage-01 shows 6/16 **capacity slots**, not 9 installed.
- Never show Chemical Analysis results, cage report output or underwater pressure telemetry as currently functioning.
- Validate lifting/boat/crane capabilities independently; the storyboard is not an engineering lift plan.
- Distinguish "locked visual reference" from "final animated shot" in file naming and QA.
